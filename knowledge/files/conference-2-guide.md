# IT Infrastructure & Operations Summit — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Infrastructure  
**Location:** Las Vegas  
**Dates:** 2026-03-05 to 2026-03-07 (3 days)  
**Sessions:** 18

## Overview

The IT Infrastructure & Operations Summit is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for infrastructure leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote
