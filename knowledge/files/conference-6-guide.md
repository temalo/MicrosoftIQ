# CIO Leadership Forum — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Executive Leadership  
**Location:** Phoenix  
**Dates:** 2026-07-11 to 2026-07-12 (2 days)  
**Sessions:** 12

## Overview

The CIO Leadership Forum is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for executive leadership leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote

**Opening Keynote: The Future of Executive Leadership** — delivered by Leila Suzuki in Hall B.
