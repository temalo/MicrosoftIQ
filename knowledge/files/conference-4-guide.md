# Supply Chain Symposium — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Supply Chain  
**Location:** Barcelona  
**Dates:** 2026-05-08 to 2026-05-10 (3 days)  
**Sessions:** 16

## Overview

The Supply Chain Symposium is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for supply chain leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote

**Executive Keynote: The Future of Supply Chain** — delivered by Hugo Yilmaz in Hall E.
