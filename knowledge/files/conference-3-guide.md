# Security & Risk Summit — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Security  
**Location:** National Harbor  
**Dates:** 2026-04-06 to 2026-04-08 (3 days)

## Overview

The Security & Risk Summit is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for security leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.
