# Digital Workplace Summit — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Employee Experience  
**Location:** Amsterdam  
**Dates:** 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-14 (2 days)  
**Sessions:** 12

## Overview

The Digital Workplace Summit is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for employee experience leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote

**Vision Keynote: The Future of Employee Experience** — delivered by Tariq Popov in Hall C.
