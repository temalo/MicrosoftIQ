# Contoso Events Sponsorship Prospectus

Contoso Events conferences connect sponsoring technology providers with senior decision-makers across data, security, infrastructure, supply chain and marketing. This prospectus describes the available packages and the current sponsor roster.

## Sponsorship packages

Contoso Events offers five sponsorship packages per event:

### Platinum — $250,000 per event
- Premium exhibition booth in the prime expo location
- A mainstage keynote or premium breakout speaking slot
- Expo listing, mobile-app placement and lead scanning
- 10 full-conference passes
- Logo on mainstage and event signage

### Gold — $120,000 per event
- Exhibition booth in a high-traffic aisle
- A breakout speaking slot
- Expo listing, mobile-app placement and lead scanning
- 6 full-conference passes

### Silver — $55,000 per event
- Standard exhibition booth
- Expo listing and lead scanning
- 3 full-conference passes

### Bronze — $22,000 per event
- Shared exhibition kiosk
- Expo listing
- 2 full-conference passes

### Startup — $8,000 per event
- Startup-alley kiosk
- Expo listing
- 1 full-conference pass

## Value and ROI

Sponsors are measured on leads captured and qualified, influenced pipeline and closed-won revenue attributed to the event.

> **Top value driver:** Datallar has generated the most influenced pipeline across the series (see the Fabric data for exact figures and ROI).
