# Application Innovation Summit — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Software Engineering  
**Location:** Toronto  
**Dates:** 2026-08-12 to 2026-08-14 (3 days)  
**Sessions:** 16

## Overview

The Application Innovation Summit is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for software engineering leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote

**Vision Keynote: The Future of Software Engineering** — delivered by Ines Popov in Hall C.
