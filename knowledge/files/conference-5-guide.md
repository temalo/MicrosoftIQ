# Marketing & CX Conference — Conference Guide

**Theme:** Marketing  
**Location:** Denver  
**Dates:** 2026-06-09 to 2026-06-10 (2 days)  
**Sessions:** 14

## Overview

The Marketing & CX Conference is Contoso Events's flagship gathering for marketing leaders and practitioners. Expect a mainstage keynote, deep-dive breakouts, hands-on workshops and peer roundtables, plus an expo of sponsoring technology providers.

## Opening keynote

**Executive Keynote: The Future of Marketing** — delivered by Rosa Weber in Hall A.
